Why this partnership publication exists

Business partners need a shared understanding of how their agreement, cash movements and tax reporting interact. A partner's capital account, ownership percentage and outside tax basis are different measures. A useful planning conversation identifies which measure controls the decision before money is contributed, distributed or borrowed.

Start with the outside-basis example, then read the distribution and liability guides. For an ownership purchase, use the Section 754 guide alongside the transaction documents. These resources help readers prepare focused questions and organize the facts an advisor needs.

What to bring to a partnership planning discussion

  • The signed operating agreement and all amendments.
  • Recent Forms 1065, Schedules K-1 and partner basis workpapers.
  • Contribution, distribution, debt and ownership-transfer records.
  • The proposed transaction and the partners' intended economic result.
