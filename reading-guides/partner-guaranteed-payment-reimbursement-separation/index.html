Separate guaranteed payments and expense reimbursements in partner records

Trace each partner payment to what it covers so cash totals do not obscure reporting differences.

Worked example

In one month a partner receives $3,000 for services and $300 for a documented travel expense. Match the service payment to the compensation agreement and match the expense payment to its receipt and approval. Confirm the reporting of both with the partnership preparer.

What to document

Keep the agreement, receipts, approvals, and ledger detail together. Ask the preparer to reconcile the service-payment schedule to the K-1 and document how the expense line is treated.
