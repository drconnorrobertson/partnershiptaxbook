Reconcile a change in partnership liabilities on Schedule K-1

Identify whether a change in the partner’s liability share affects the outside-basis computation.

Worked example

The same partner’s reported share drops from $18,000 to $12,000. Reconcile the $6,000 difference to the loan and allocation records. An applicable decrease generally is treated as a distribution of money for basis purposes, subject to the circumstances.

What to document

Check the opening and ending shares, the date and cause of the change, and any related cash distribution. Send the bridge to the preparer for the basis and distribution review.
