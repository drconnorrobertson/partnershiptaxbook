Document a partner cash contribution in the basis worksheet

Connect a cash contribution to the partnership ledger and the contributing partner’s basis record.

Worked example

A partner sends $15,000 and the ledger labels it an owner loan. Compare the signed agreement, bank transfer and partnership books before moving the amount to the contribution line. A genuine cash contribution generally increases outside basis.

What to document

Record the amount, date, paying party, reason and governing approval. Keep any loan agreement or contribution support so the preparer can resolve conflicting labels.
