Reconcile a partnership cash distribution from bank to Schedule K-1

Match partner payments to the ledger and determine how much belongs in the distribution review.

Worked example

The bank shows three transfers of $4,000 to one partner. The ledger codes two as distributions and one as a reimbursement. Review the underlying invoice and approval before treating the full $12,000 as distributed cash.

What to document

Tie the confirmed distributions to the partner statement and outside-basis worksheet. Keep the unmatched transfers visible and check separately for relevant liability changes.
