Review year-end timing for guaranteed payments to a partner

Reconcile the partnership tax year, the payment records and the reported amount before assuming the reporting year.

Worked example

A partnership with a fiscal year sends cash to a partner in December, but the partnership tax year closes in January. Have the preparer match the payment agreement and ledger to the relevant partnership tax year and Schedule K-1 before assigning it to a personal return.

What to document

Record the partnership year end, payment date, month of service and reported K-1 figure. Flag any timing difference between the bank records and the tax reporting workpaper.
