Rebuild a missing partnership outside-basis opening balance

Trace an opening outside-basis figure to prior records before carrying it into a distribution or loss review.

Worked example

A partner’s current worksheet starts at $48,000 but the prior signed worksheet ends at $43,000. Look for a documented later contribution, revised Schedule K-1 or corrected liability share before accepting the difference.

What to document

Build a bridge from the last supported balance to the opening number. Record the date, amount, source and treatment for each movement. If documentation is missing, leave the amount open for review.
